What CAS is & why the last 15 minutes move "randomly"

Closing Auction (CAS): the day's close is set by a short call-auction around 15:15–15:40 IST (mostly just before 15:30). Big desks concentrate their closing orders here. You've seen Sensex drop 2000 pts / Nifty jump 200 in this window while no stock moves that much — that's the tell: it's option/derivative positioning, not the cash basket.

The footprint we read (the logic)
  • FII index-futures long/short — net short (low L/S ratio) = bearish; covering shorts = bullish. The biggest tell.
  • FII options tilt — long PUTS + short CALLS = bearish hedge; the reverse = bullish.
  • FII & DII cash flows — who's accumulating/distributing; DII buying often puts a floor under FII selling.
  • Max Pain — the strike where option WRITERS lose least; price is pulled there into the close (the "target").
  • Gamma regime — above the flip dealers SUPPRESS (pin/range); below it they AMPLIFY (a wall-break trends hard — that's your 2000-pt day).

We combine these into a daily BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL bias + conviction + the level they're targeting (Positioning tab).

How the forecast is built (the basis)

Expected close = current spot + a small drift in the direction of the current FII/DII bias (capped). The range = spot × daily-volatility × √(days out) — a standard uncertainty cone that widens with time. Near-term is anchored to today's option positioning; months out is trend + volatility only. No one can name December's exact close — so we show the expected path AND the honest range, and roll it forward every day.

⚙️ THE EXACT FORMULAS (nothing hidden)

1. Max Pain — for every strike K: loss(K) = Σ (K−strike)·callOI for strikes below K + Σ (strike−K)·putOI for strikes above K. Max Pain = the K with the smallest loss (where writers lose least). It's the pin target.

2. Pin lean — dist% = (MaxPain − Spot)/Spot ×100. |dist%|<0.12 → PIN · >0 → PULL UP · <0 → PULL DOWN.

3. Gamma flip — walk strikes low→high summing net OI (callOI−putOI); the strike where the running sum crosses 0 = flip. Spot < flip → NEG GAMMA (dealers amplify → trend/2000-pt days) · Spot ≥ flip → POS GAMMA (dealers pin/range).

4. Footprint score (the daily bias) — start 0, then add:

  • pin PULL UP +1 · PULL DOWN −1 · PIN 0
  • FII futures L/S ratio ≤0.5 → −1.6 · ≥1.5 → +1.6
  • FII options: long PUTS+short CALLS −1 · long CALLS+short PUTS +1
  • FII cash ≥+₹800cr +0.6 · ≤−₹800cr −0.6
  • DII cash ≥+₹1500cr +0.5 · ≤−₹1500cr −0.5

score ≥ +1 → BULLISH · ≤ −1 → BEARISH · else NEUTRAL. Conviction = min(88, 48 + |score|×10)%.

5. Forecast — for each trading day N ahead: drift = clamp(score × 0.0015 × N, −12%, +12%) · Expected = Spot ×(1+drift) · Range = Spot × volDaily × √N (volDaily: Nifty/Sensex 0.7%, BankNifty 0.9%).

6. Historical prediction (FII/DII only, since option-OI wasn't stored): futNet<−100k −1 · >+100k +1 · covering(+20k vs prior) +0.6 · adding shorts(−20k) −0.6 · FIIcash±800 ±0.5 · DIIcash>2500 +0.3. score≥0.5→UP · ≤−0.5→DOWN. Scored vs next-day Nifty move (±0.15% = the flat band).

These weights are hand-set heuristics from trading logic, not curve-fitted — that's why they're transparent and adjustable, and why the measured hit-rate is the honest judge.
