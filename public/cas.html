Why the last 15 minutes move "randomly" — and why it isn't random

The close is set by a call-auction around 15:15–15:30 IST, where big desks concentrate their closing orders. You've seen Sensex drop 2000 pts / Nifty jump 200 in this window while no single stock moves that much — that's the tell: it's derivative/dealer positioning, not the cash basket. It IS mechanical. Three forces set it, and we measure all three:

① Max-pain PIN strength — the gravity

Max Pain = the strike where option writers lose the least. Writers (big desks) defend it, so price is pulled there into the close. But the pin only bites when OI is concentrated near it and expiry is near. We quantify it: pinStrength = (OI within ±2 strikes of max pain / total OI) × expiryFactor × 260, where expiryFactor = 1 on expiry day, 0.75 (1d), 0.5 (≤3d), 0.32 (≤7d), 0.18 (far). A strong pin on expiry day → the index sits on max pain at 15:30.

② Dealer GAMMA — suppress or amplify

Walk the strikes low→high summing net OI (callOI−putOI); the strike where the running sum crosses 0 is the gamma flip. Spot ≥ flip → POSITIVE gamma: dealers hedge AGAINST the move (buy weakness / sell strength) → they suppress → quiet pinned close. Spot < flip → NEGATIVE gamma: dealers hedge WITH the move (sell weakness / buy strength) → they amplify → this is the regime behind the sudden 1–2% closing swings. gammaMag = dealer pressure near ATM, 0–100.

③ Live OI FLOW — who's pressing into the close

From intraday OI snapshots (oi_history): rising call OI = call writing (cap building, bearish); falling call OI = call covering (squeeze up, bullish); rising put OI = put writing (floor building, bullish); falling put OI = put covering (support pulled, bearish). flowBias = (ΔputOI − ΔcallOI) / baseOI → the intraday pressure the old model was blind to.

④ The regime decision (how the call is made)
  • POS gamma & pinStrength≥45 & |dist|<0.25% → PINNED · expected = max pain · tight band · conf up to 85
  • NEG gamma → VOLATILE · expected = spot + amplified move in the tilt direction · wide band · conf ≤68 (we call direction+size, not an exact number)
  • POS gamma & |dist|≥0.25% → MAGNET · expected = spot + 45% of the way to max pain
  • else → TILTED · mild lean from flow + FII

tilt = pin-pull ± OI-flow(0.8) ± FII-futures(0.9) ± FII/DII-cash → sets direction & feeds confidence. Everything is capped; confidence never claims 90%+.

The headline number up top is the measured directional hit-rate — did the close move the way the footprint called?
